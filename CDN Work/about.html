OUR STORY

Born from a Passion
for Excellence

AutoLux was founded in 2010 by a team of automotive enthusiasts who believed that buying a luxury car should be as exhilarating as driving one. We started with a small showroom and five iconic brands — today we represent over 20 of the world's most prestigious manufacturers.

Our mission is simple: bring together the finest vehicles on the planet and pair them with an unmatched, white-glove ownership experience. Every car in our collection is hand-selected, meticulously inspected, and presented with full transparency.
